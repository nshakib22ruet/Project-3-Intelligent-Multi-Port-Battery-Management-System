Project 3

ESP32-Based Intelligent Multi-Port Battery Management System

Md. Najmus Shakib

Department of Electrical & Electronic Engineering (EEE), RUET

Overview

An ESP32-based system that charges and supervises multiple batteries simultaneously. It measures voltage, current, and temperature across each charging path, switches paths through relays in manual or automatic mode, and streams live telemetry to a web dashboard. A regression-based machine learning model evaluates temperature rise and electrical parameters to classify battery health and detect degradation.

About the Project

The system takes 220 V AC mains as its primary power source. A step-down transformer lowers the voltage, a bridge rectifier converts it to DC, and a filter capacitor smooths the rectified output. An L7805CV regulator supplies 5 V to the 4-channel relay module, while an LM2596 buck converter regulates charging voltage for Battery Port 2.

The ESP32 acts as the central controller, operating relay channels under manual or automatic safety thresholds. Dual INA219 sensors measure voltage and current on separate I2C buses, while two DS18B20 sensors monitor battery temperature.

The firmware estimates state-of-charge, charging current, internal resistance under valid load conditions, and thermal rate of rise. These parameters feed a regression model for battery health assessment and automated safety cutoffs.

A real-time web dashboard displays port status, voltage, current, temperature, estimated battery health, and relay states over Bluetooth/serial telemetry.
